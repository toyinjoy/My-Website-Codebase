About

Hi, I'm Oluwatoyin Joy Omole.

I'm a data analyst and BI developer with 5 years of experience turning raw data into decisions. I've built dashboards, data models and data pipelines for microfinance institutions, commercial banks, an electricity company, insurance and transport clients, and government agencies.

Now I bring that same work to small businesses. Most owners I meet have plenty of data; it just lives in five places and takes days to pull together. I fix that at the root: connect the sources, agree the numbers that matter, and hand over dashboards your team can run on their own.

Business intelligence is where I start. Machine learning is where I'm heading: I hold a Master of Science in Computer Science and already use Python for predictive modelling and customer segmentation.
